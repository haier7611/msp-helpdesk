# Client guide

How to get help from your IT support team.

## Raise a ticket

You can do either:

- **Send an email** to your support address. The subject becomes the ticket title; attach any
  screenshots or files.
- **Use the portal**: sign in, choose **New ticket**, pick the type of request, describe the problem
  and how much it affects you, add screenshots if you can, and press **Send**.

You get a reference such as `ACD-042` straight away, on screen and by email.

### Signing in to the portal

Use your email and password, or **Email me a sign-in link** (the link works once, for 15 minutes),
or **Sign in with Microsoft** if your company uses it. The portal is only for raising tickets; you
follow them by email.

## Follow your ticket by email

- Every update from the support team comes by email, with your reference in the subject, for
  example `[ACD-042]`.
- To add information, a screenshot or an answer, just **reply to that email**.
- **Keep `[ACD-042]` in the subject** when you reply or forward. That is how your message finds its
  ticket. Without it, it may start a new ticket.
- To bring a colleague in, copy them on your reply. Colleagues from your company are added
  automatically; people outside it are added once the support team agrees.

## When we are waiting for you

If the team asks you a question, the ticket waits for your answer and you get a reminder. If we do
not hear back, the ticket is resolved after a few working days. A reply at any time reopens it.

## When it is fixed

You get an email when your ticket is resolved. If it is not fixed, reply within 5 days and it
reopens. After that, a reply starts a new ticket linked to the old one.

## Tips

- One problem per ticket.
- Say what you expected and what happened instead, and when it started.
- For something urgent that stops several people working, say so in the subject, and in the
  portal choose "Whole office" and "Work stopped".
- Never send passwords by email. The support team will ask you safely if they need one.
